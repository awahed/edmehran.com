Inside the book

A taste of how the prompts work.

Every prompt runs inside a Claude AI Project. The more context your Project accumulates, the sharper the output. Here is how the system shifts from dictation to direction.

Prompt 2 - The Thinking Partner Activation

From this point forward in this Project, I need you to operate as my Thinking Partner. Not an assistant. Not a yes-machine. Not a text generator. Your role is to challenge my assumptions, surface risks I have not considered, and push back when my logic is weak.

When I present an idea, analyse it first. When I ask for options, rank them and explain why. When I am wrong, say so directly.

This is the prompt that changes the relationship. After this, Claude stops being polite and starts being useful.

What you give Claude AI

Permission to challenge you. Your venture context, assumptions, and the decisions keeping you up at night.

What Claude AI gives you

Direct pushback, ranked options with reasoning, and the analysis you would get from a senior advisor, without the invoice.

Common questions

About the book and using Claude AI for venture building.

What is The Venture Builder's AI Playbook?

It is a practical book containing 18 structured prompts for Claude AI that guide founders through every stage of launching a venture: market research, business planning, pitch deck creation, validation sprints, and building a personal AI operating system. Written by Ed Mehran, Fractional CTO and founder based in Switzerland.

How does Claude AI help with business planning and venture building?

Most people use Claude AI like a typist, dictating what to write. This book teaches you to direct it like a thinking partner. With the right prompts and a structured Project, Claude analyses markets, pressure-tests assumptions, builds financial frameworks, crafts investor-ready pitch decks, and runs validation sprints. The difference is not the tool. It is how you use it.

Who is this book for?

The book is written for the person who runs things: founders launching new ventures, experienced entrepreneurs entering new markets, and innovation leads inside companies who need to make the business case. It is especially relevant for the Swiss and DACH market, where business culture rewards transparency about assumptions and evidence-based decisions over hype.

Do I need technical skills to use the 18 prompts?

No. The prompts are designed for business founders and executives, not engineers. You need a Claude AI account and the willingness to think carefully about your venture. The book walks you through every prompt, explains what it produces, and shows you how to use the output.

What will I have after completing the book?

You will have five complete deliverables: a market intelligence document, a business plan with tested assumptions, an investor-ready pitch deck, validation sprint results with a clear go/no-go decision, and a personal AI operating system with a weekly executive review habit. All built inside a Claude AI Project that compounds in value over time.
